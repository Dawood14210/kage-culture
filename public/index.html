Policy

Return & Refund Policy

Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.

Eligible Items

  • ✓ All clothing items (tees, compression, oversized)
  • ✓ Items in original condition with tags
  • ✗ Sale items (final sale unless defective)
  • ✗ Custom-designed items and custom stickers (non-returnable unless defective or wrong)
  • ✗ Worn, washed, or damaged items

How to Request a Return

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp at +92 322 804 2633 with your order number
  2. We'll confirm eligibility and provide return instructions
  3. Ship the item back (return shipping paid by customer unless item is defective)
  4. Refund processed within 3-5 business days of receiving the return

Refund Method

Refunds are issued via bank transfer to the account you specify. COD orders are refunded via bank transfer only.

Defective Items

If you received a defective or wrong item, contact us within 48 hours with photos. We'll cover return shipping and send a replacement or full refund.

Our Story

KAGE

影 — KAGE CULTURE

Every shadow starts with something standing in the light.

We started in a single room with one idea: shirts that said something, worn by people who didn't need to explain themselves. No committee decided the first print. No trend calendar told us when to drop it.

We drew what we'd actually wear. We printed it on fabric heavy enough to survive more than one season. We didn't ask permission.

A brand isn't built in a boardroom. It's built one shirt, one customer, one late night getting the ink right, at a time. We're still in that room. We just have more people watching now.
